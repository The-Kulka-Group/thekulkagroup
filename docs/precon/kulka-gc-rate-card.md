# Kulka General Conditions rate card

Answers LA-688. Staffing and field-office rates only, pulled from Kulka's own estimate
for 117 N. Long Beach Rd (estimate `C-00379`, revision 1), file `117 N Long Beach Rd
GC's REV1.xlsx`, `General Conditions (GC-CM)` tab. Original submission dated
2023-07-19; the tab's own header also carries a "REVISION #0 Submitted 7-19-24" note a
year later, an inconsistency in the source file itself, not resolved here, flagged
rather than picked.

**One real caveat before this becomes a benchmark: this is a single job, and it is not
multifamily.** 117 N. Long Beach Rd is a 120,000 SF single-story warehouse, a 112,650 SF
warehouse floor plus two small flanking offices, dock levelers, trailer landing pads,
heavy-duty asphalt. Staffing roles and field-office setup costs are the kind of overhead
that travels reasonably well across building types, the same superintendent and PM sit
in the same trailer regardless of what's being built. The dollar total this job's GC
rolled up to, $727,230, 5.96% of a $12.2M hardcost, is not a multifamily benchmark and
should not be read as one. It answers "what does a role or a trailer cost," not "what
percent should a multifamily GC run."

This file does not answer LA-688's other two questions: whether rates vary by job size
(n=1 here, no comparison point), and General Requirements coverage. The same estimate
carries a separate `General Requirements (GC- CM)` tab, temp heat, temp utilities,
hoisting, and similar Division 01 scope, not pulled here since this pass was scoped to
staffing and field offices only. That tab is real and available whenever GR is worth the
same treatment.

## Job-level GC roll-up, for reference only, not a benchmark

Construction-phase staffing and logistics totaled $663,076 on this job, field offices
and expenses $61,154, general expenses $3,000, for a $727,230 General Conditions total
against a $12,195,050 hardcost subtotal, 5.96%. `rom-system-design.md` already carries a
7-to-8-percent range for combined GC and GR from the six multifamily ProEst files; this
job's 5.96% GC-only figure sits inside that range but isn't additive to it and shouldn't
be blended with it, different denominator, different building type, different scope
(GC only here, GC-plus-GR there).
