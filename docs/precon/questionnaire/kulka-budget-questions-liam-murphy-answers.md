# Kulka budget questions: answers from Liam Murphy

Completed 2026-09-18. 19 of 19 answered.

## Theme 1: the files we have

### Q1. Is "ILNW Building 1" in ProEst the same scope as "NWIL" in Autodesk?

It is not the same scope, it is very similar but the one on Autodesk is the final version of the scope. The one on ProEst is conceptual. Autodesk has the 100% drawings

### Q2. Which of the three Vue budgets in Riverhead is the current one?

The $11.6M one is the most up to date, current one

### Q3. What changed between those three Vue budgets?

design changes

### Q4. Are all three Vue budgets the same project?

Yes, they are the same project

### Q5. What scope does the Villa at Glen Cove's $250 per square foot cover?

that scope covers the complete construction of each individual building

### Q6. Is the West Shore Road export the whole budget for that job?

no that is not the whole budget, it is missing mechanical, electrical, and finishes costs. it is partial

### Q7. Are these two West Shore Road lines errors?

yes, the one at $125 is an error. The correct sanitary sewer allowance is the $125,000. drainage allowance of $550,004.67 is a correct allowance

## Theme 2: cost codes and how work gets grouped

### Q8. Is there a house standard for cost codes in ProEst?

yes, there is a house standard for cost codes in ProEst. It can be found in the Database tab on ProEst

### Q9. Where should light gauge metal framing sit, Division 06 or Division 09?

It should be in Division 09

### Q10. Does a single cost code ever hold both building work and site work?

very rarely does a single cost code hold both building work and site work. we try to break them out, example: site concrete vs. building concrete

### Q11. When you sanity-check a budget number, do you think in divisions or in buyout packages?

we think in divisions

## Theme 3: what the tool should ask

### Q12. Which of those eleven can you not answer about a past job?

i can answer all 11 of those

### Q13. Is unit mix knowable when a developer first calls?

50/50. it depends how far along the design development is on the project. some of the projects we bid are issued to bid drawings while some are very conceptual

### Q14. Is structure type known when a developer first calls?

yes, most of the time it is known. if it is not known, we use our expertise and opinion (usually only for very conceptual budgets where to owner does not know what they want to do/hasn't engaged an architect/engineer to provide that guidance yet)

### Q15. What is missing from those eleven?

i don't think anything is missing

## Theme 4: markups, presentation, and trust

### Q16. What moves the markup percentages on a conceptual budget?

it is usually driven by owner-request or level of the drawings. the more conceptual the project is (aka the more design development needed), the higher our contingency is

### Q17. How do developers want a conceptual budget broken down?

we provide developers conceptual budgets broken down into divisions

### Q18. What do developers push back on, and what do they ask you to prove?

if they push back, they are usually asking for more detail (cost breakouts)

### Q19. What would make you not trust a number this tool produced?

the number would have to be very far off (too high or too low) from what we've experience, whether that be past projects or standard cost per SF we know offhand/in our heads
