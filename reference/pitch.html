Four directions

The current build scores 68. The structure is right and the skin is borrowed. This pitch holds the information architecture still and varies everything the eye judges: palette, type scale, shape, density, and finish. Each direction is complete enough to decide on: tokens, buttons, inputs, status chips, navigation, and the same Pursuits board rendered in that direction's hand.

All names, clients, and dates below are example data. Status chips set their labels in ink on bright tints with a vivid dot, so the color stays loud and every label passes AA contrast.

What stays fixed in every direction

These are owner decisions and gauntlet laws. The directions compete on top of them, never against them.

Open Sans, four weightsThe client's brand family. 400 reads, 600 acts, 700 heads, 800 speaks. The directions differ in scale and use, never in family.
The IA holdsHome, Pursuits, Clients, Briefs, with spoken sheet numbers. A user who says "open P-101" is using the product correctly.
One pill treatmentStatus is a bright tinted pill with a vivid dot and an ink label, at a fixed width. Hue carries state and marks nothing else on a board.
Red is tieredRed means a deadline is blown. Amber means a decision is waiting. Late dates read as bold ink because the pill already carries the color.
Attention in sentencesHome leads with what needs you, one line per item, one action each. When nothing does, the screen says so plainly.
No costumeThe trade vocabulary lives in the words, where your users speak it. Nothing below dresses up as a blueprint, a drawing set, or a job site.

The recommendation

Ship Daylight and prototype Linen. Daylight is Revision B completed, needs no owner re-approval, and can be in the product this week. Linen is the only direction with an identity nobody else can wear, so it earns one fully built screen and a blind gauntlet against Daylight before the CRM's public debut.

The other two donate their best parts. Porcelain's header scale and pill focus rings fold into whichever direction wins. Slate becomes a compact density toggle rather than a direction of its own, which serves the heavy operator without betting the brand on her.

The test protocol stays the one your design doc already trusts: build the Pursuits board for both finalists on real data shapes, place each beside the Monday board, and judge blind with three people who were not in this room. The winner ships, and the rules it proves get written into DESIGN.md as Revision C.
