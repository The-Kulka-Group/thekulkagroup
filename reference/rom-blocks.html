The four levels behind a ROM

Gross square feet is level one, not the whole system. Pricing moves through four levels of detail, never past what the estimator actually gave it.

Sample request, as spokenWe want to build a 200-unit building with 150 three-bed two-full-bath units, 35 two-bed one-full-bath one-powder-room units, and 15 studios with one full bath. 70 units will have walk-out balconies. Two buildings, each with two elevators. Building 1 kitchens get pot fillers. Building 1 full baths get two sinks.

Accepted only if a file or a sub’s own quote already carries it. Nothing here is guessed at just to fill the level.

A level-2 or level-3 line can be a breakout of a level-1 lump, not an add-on. Kulka’s own electrical files already price elevator wiring inside the one flat $/SF lump. A level-2 “elevator wiring” block carved out of that lump doesn’t add to the total — it only becomes a real addition once a benchmark exists net of it.
